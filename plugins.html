MOTION DOCK

After Effects 2026 / Windows / v2.8.1

Keyframes, shapes, expressions and exports in one dockable panel.

Download ZXP ↘
Installation & updates
  1. Close After Effects and install the ZXP with aescripts ZXP/UXP Installer.
  2. Restart After Effects, then open Window → Extensions (Legacy) → Motion Dock.
  3. For new versions, choose Updates → Install in the panel, then Reload panel.
